Our Commitment

What Makes It Truly Zabiha

Not all halal is created equal. Zabiha halal means every animal is hand-slaughtered by a Muslim — no shortcuts, no machines.

01
🤲

Bismillah Recited

A trained Muslim slaughterer recites the name of Allah before every single animal — this is the foundation of zabiha halal.

02
🌿

Humanely Raised

Animals are raised in humane conditions, free to graze and eat clean. Islam commands kindness to animals at every stage.

03
⚡

Swift, Hand-Cut

The cut is swift and precise with a sharp blade — minimizing suffering as commanded by the Prophet ﷺ.

04
🩸

Blood Fully Drained

Complete drainage of blood is required and ensured every time — a health and religious requirement of halal.

05
✅

No Cross-Contamination

Equipment, surfaces, and storage are kept strictly separate from any non-halal products.

06
📦

Fresh to Your Door

Packaged fresh and sealed with care. Pick up in-store or arrange local delivery — no long waits, no old stock.
